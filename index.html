Our Advisory Services

Where capital and resources meet, we build the bridge.

African Capital Access

We help African businesses and project developers access US institutional capital. This includes structuring DFC and Exim Bank facilities, preparing investor grade documentation, and positioning projects to meet the due diligence standards of US government backed lenders and development finance institutions.

Critical Minerals Origination

We source and facilitate access to verified supply of copper cathodes, bauxite, iron ore, platinum group metals, lithium, chromium, and graphite from producing operations across West and Southern Africa. US offtakers engage us when they need a credible, principal led route to supply.

Commodity Trade Facilitation

We connect verified commodity buyers directly to qualified producers. Our introductions are structured, our counterparties are credentialed, and our transaction frameworks eliminate unnecessary intermediary layers. From FCO to SPA, we support both sides through to execution. Alongside our mineral origination work, we facilitate select petroleum product transactions, including Jet A-1, drawing on established relationships with major producers in the Middle East.
